i ~wrote~ asked cursor to write a component that displays the latest cleveland cavaliers game i’ve watched (we can safely assume i watch every game). a [vercel function](https://vercel.com/docs/functions) at [`/api/cavaliers`](https://github.com/skullface/skullface.me/blob/main/src/pages/api/cavaliers.js) hits [JSON from the NBA](https://cdn.nba.com/static/json/staticData/scheduleLeagueV2.json), walks `leagueSchedule.gameDates` to get every finished cavs game (noting home vs. away), sorts them in chrono desc order by `gameDateTimeUTC`, and then takes the most recent game.

the result renders team names and scores in the expected, human-readable order with an editorial suffix, e.g., **cavs @ suns (113 – 126) oof** or **lakers @ cavs (99–129) w!**. the response is cached for 24 hours as there’s never more than one game per day.
